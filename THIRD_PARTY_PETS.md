# Third-party pet assets

The MIT license in this repository applies to the DeskPet application and integration code only. It does **not** relicense bundled pet artwork, characters, names, or trademarks.

## Built-in and bundled sources

- **BongoCat** — adapted from [ayangweb/bongocat](https://github.com/ayangweb/bongocat), whose source repository is MIT licensed.
- **Anya and Codex Anime Pets** — from [chenxin-dlut/codex-anime-pets](https://github.com/chenxin-dlut/codex-anime-pets). Its asset terms specify personal, non-commercial desktop-pet use.
- **Shen Xinghui, Bond Forger, Luna Angel Cat** — downloaded through [awesome-codex-pet](https://github.com/legeling/awesome-codex-pet); each asset remains subject to its source metadata and rights holder.
- **Codex Pets packages** — downloaded from [codex-pets.net](https://codex-pets.net/); artwork remains owned by its original creator or rights holder.
- **PetDex packages** — downloaded from [petdex.dev](https://petdex.dev/); artwork remains subject to the package or creator's terms.

The bundled collection currently includes BongoCat plus 30 locally installed packages. Their original `pet.json` files are preserved where available so source identifiers and preview URLs remain inspectable.

## Network catalogs

The app can browse and install compatible packages from:

- [Awesome Codex Pet](https://github.com/legeling/awesome-codex-pet)
- [Codex Pets](https://codex-pets.net/)
- [PetDex](https://petdex.dev/)
- [SpriteYard](https://www.spriteyard.com/)
- [AgentBro Pet Market](https://www.agentbro.net/pets)
- [OpenPets](https://openpets.dev/gallery)
- [Codex Anime Pets](https://github.com/chenxin-dlut/codex-anime-pets)

Catalog inclusion means technical compatibility, not an ownership claim or endorsement. Before publishing a public fork that includes downloaded assets, verify redistribution rights for every bundled package or remove `app/BundledPets/` and let users download pets from their original sources.
